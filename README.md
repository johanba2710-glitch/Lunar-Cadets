Lunar Cadets — J&R AstroLabs
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 | Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer
Lunar Cadets is an interactive, space-themed educational mission simulator developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. It is designed to introduce students to the challenges of becoming an astronaut and managing a sustainable lunar outpost through an engaging, game-based experience.

The experience takes players through a simulated astronaut selection and training journey before placing them in charge of a lunar mission. Players must make critical decisions, manage limited resources, maintain life-support systems, handle mission challenges, and keep their lunar outpost operational.

Key Features
Astronaut Selection: An interactive fictional astronaut application and selection experience.

Astronaut Training: Prepare for a simulated space mission through training activities.

Lunar Landing: Experience the challenges of reaching and landing on the Moon.

Lunar Outpost Management: Manage essential resources and maintain a functioning lunar base.

Life Support Systems: Understand the importance of oxygen, CO₂ management, and crew survival.

Rover Exploration: Explore the lunar environment and collect scientific samples.

Mission Challenges: Experience unexpected situations and make decisions that affect mission progress.

Mission Reports: Review mission performance and evaluate the outcome of a completed mission.

Astra AI: An AI-powered guidance assistant that helps players understand the simulator and their mission objectives.

Project Objective
Our goal is to make space exploration and astronautics more accessible to students by transforming complex space-mission concepts into an interactive learning experience. Instead of simply reading about space missions, players learn by making decisions and experiencing their consequences.
